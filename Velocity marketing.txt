Retail Marketing: We help brands strengthen their retail presence by delivering consistent in-store execution, enhancing product visibility, and creating engaging shopping experiences across General Trade, Modern Trade, and Organized Retail.

a) Merchandising: Merchandising is the process of presenting products in an attractive and organized manner to maximize sales and improve the shopping experience. It involves placing the right products in the right locations at the right time to attract customer attention. Effective merchandising ensures products are well-stocked, correctly priced, and displayed according to brand guidelines. It also includes promotional displays, seasonal arrangements, and inventory monitoring. In Velocity Marketing, merchandising teams regularly visit retail stores to maintain display standards, improve product visibility, and ensure compliance with retailer requirements. Good merchandising increases impulse purchases, strengthens brand presence, enhances customer satisfaction, and contributes to higher sales and market share.
b) Shelf Audits: Shelf audits are systematic inspections conducted in retail stores to evaluate product availability, display quality, pricing accuracy, and shelf conditions. The objective is to ensure that products are stocked correctly and comply with brand and retailer standards. During a shelf audit, field teams check stock levels, identify out-of-stock items, verify promotional materials, and monitor competitor activities. They also assess shelf cleanliness and product placement. In Velocity Marketing, shelf audits provide valuable market insights that help brands improve execution, prevent lost sales, and maintain strong retail visibility. Regular audits support better inventory planning and enhance overall retail performance.
c) Planogram Execution: Planogram execution is the implementation of a predefined store layout that specifies how products should be arranged on shelves. A planogram helps maximize product visibility, improve customer navigation, and optimize shelf space utilization. Field teams ensure products are placed in the correct sequence, facing, and quantity as per brand guidelines. They also verify pricing labels and promotional placements. In Velocity Marketing, accurate planogram execution ensures consistency across retail outlets, strengthens brand identity, improves shopping convenience, and increases product sales through strategic shelf positioning.
d) POP/POSM Installation: POP (Point of Purchase) and POSM (Point of Sale Materials) installation involves placing promotional materials in retail outlets to attract shoppers and increase brand awareness. These materials include danglers, shelf strips, posters, wobblers, standees, display units, banners, and digital screens. The installation is carried out according to brand guidelines to maximize customer engagement. Velocity Marketing ensures proper placement, timely installation, and regular maintenance of POP/POSM materials. Effective execution enhances product visibility, supports promotional campaigns, influences buying decisions, and improves the overall retail shopping experience.
e) Retail Branding: Retail branding focuses on creating a consistent and appealing brand presence within retail stores to improve customer recognition and trust. It includes branded displays, signboards, shelf branding, promotional zones, digital screens, and visual communication that reflect the brand's identity. Velocity Marketing works with retailers to ensure branding elements are professionally installed and maintained across multiple locations. Strong retail branding differentiates products from competitors, enhances store aesthetics, reinforces brand recall, and encourages customers to choose the brand during their purchase journey.
f) Shop Branding :Shop branding involves transforming the exterior and interior appearance of retail stores using brand-specific visual elements. This includes storefront signboards, wall graphics, window branding, entrance displays, floor stickers, and promotional panels. The objective is to create a strong visual identity that attracts customers even before they enter the store. Velocity Marketing manages the complete process from design coordination to installation and maintenance. Effective shop branding improves brand visibility, increases footfall, strengthens customer perception, and creates a lasting impression in the retail environment.
g) Visual Merchandising: Visual merchandising is the art and science of designing attractive product displays that encourage customer engagement and increase sales. It combines product placement, lighting, colors, signage, fixtures, and promotional displays to create an appealing shopping environment. Velocity Marketing develops creative in-store displays that highlight featured products, new launches, and promotional offers. Well-executed visual merchandising improves product visibility, enhances the customer experience, increases impulse purchases, and strengthens the brand image while making stores more attractive and organized.
h) Store Launch Support: Store launch support includes all operational and marketing activities required for the successful opening of a new retail outlet. Velocity Marketing assists brands by setting up product displays, executing planograms, installing POP/POSM materials, implementing branding, stocking shelves, and training store staff on merchandising standards. The team also ensures promotional campaigns are ready before the launch and monitors execution during the opening period. Effective store launch support creates a positive first impression, attracts customers, ensures operational readiness, and helps brands achieve strong initial sales and long-term retail success.











Outdoor Activations: Outdoor Activations are promotional marketing activities conducted in high-footfall public locations such as busy streets, markets, parks, beaches, bus terminals, railway stations, airports, tourist destinations, exhibitions, and public events. In Velocity Marketing, outdoor activations are designed to maximize brand visibility, engage consumers through interactive experiences, create memorable brand interactions, and generate immediate product awareness and sales opportunities.
Velocity Marketing begins every outdoor activation by understanding the client's campaign objectives, target audience, product category, and preferred locations. Based on these requirements, the company identifies suitable venues with high consumer traffic, obtains the necessary permissions from local authorities or venue management, and develops a detailed execution plan. A trained team of promoters, brand ambassadors, supervisors, and event coordinators is then deployed to manage the activation professionally.
The activation area is branded with attractive kiosks, canopies, promotional stalls, banners, standees, digital displays, and product experience zones to capture public attention. Depending on the campaign, the team conducts live product demonstrations, free sampling, interactive games, contests, lucky draws, customer registrations, QR code-based promotions, and social media engagement activities. Visitors are encouraged to interact with the brand, experience the product firsthand, and participate in promotional offers that create a memorable customer experience.

a) Roadshows: Roadshows are promotional events conducted across multiple locations to increase brand awareness and engage customers directly. They include product displays, demonstrations, entertainment, and promotional activities. Roadshows help brands reach large audiences and generate immediate customer interest. They are widely used for product launches and awareness campaigns.
b) Van Campaigns: Van campaigns use branded vehicles to promote products and services across targeted markets. These vehicles travel through cities and rural areas carrying promotional materials and product samples. They help brands reach customers directly and create strong local visibility. Van campaigns are cost-effective for regional marketing.
c) Mobile LED Van Promotions: Mobile LED van promotions use vehicles equipped with large LED screens to display advertisements, videos, and live brand messages. They travel through high-traffic areas to maximize audience reach and visibility. These campaigns attract attention through dynamic digital content. They are ideal for product launches, political campaigns, and event promotions.
d) Rural Marketing: Rural marketing focuses on promoting products and services in villages and semi-urban areas. It uses localized communication, community engagement, and field activities to reach rural consumers. Brands adopt customized strategies based on local culture and purchasing behavior. Rural marketing helps expand market penetration beyond urban regions.
e) Village Contact Programs: Village Contact Programs involve direct interaction with rural communities to educate and promote products. Activities include product demonstrations, awareness sessions, and customer meetings. These programs build trust through face-to-face communication. They help brands establish long-term relationships with rural consumers.
f) Door-to-Door Promotions: Door-to-door promotions involve sales representatives visiting households to introduce products and services personally. Teams provide product information, demonstrations, samples, and promotional offers. This approach creates direct customer engagement and immediate feedback. It is effective for building trust and increasing local sales.
g) Street Promotions: Street promotions are marketing activities conducted in busy public places such as markets, malls, and bus stations. Brand promoters interact with people through product sampling, games, contests, and demonstrations. These activities generate high visibility and encourage immediate customer participation. Street promotions effectively increase brand awareness and product trials.

Companies share new products, sales strategies, and business goals during these meetings. They also recognize top-performing dealers through awards and incentives. Dealer meets help improve motivation, loyalty, and sales performance.
b) Distributor Meets
Distributor meets are organized to engage distributors and discuss business growth opportunities. Companies review sales performance, launch new products, and explain distribution strategies. These meetings also address operational challenges and collect market feedback. Distributor meets strengthen supply chain coordination and business partnerships.
c) Retailer Meets
Retailer meets bring retailers together to introduce new products, promotional schemes, and merchandising plans. They provide training on product features and retail best practices. Companies also gather retailer feedback to improve market execution. Retailer meets enhance relationships and encourage better product placement and sales.
d) Channel Partner Events
Channel partner events are organized to build stronger relationships with dealers, distributors, retailers, and other business partners. These events include networking sessions, product launches, training, and award ceremonies. They improve collaboration and align partners with business objectives. Channel partner events help drive long-term business growth.
e) Corporate Events
Corporate events are professional gatherings organized for employees, clients, stakeholders, or business partners. They include conferences, seminars, product launches, annual meetings, and award functions. These events strengthen brand image, improve networking, and enhance business relationships. Corporate events also promote employee engagement and organizational growth.
f) Exhibitions & Trade Shows
Exhibitions and trade shows are industry events where businesses showcase their products, services, and innovations to potential customers and partners. They provide opportunities for networking, lead generation, and market research. Companies demonstrate products and interact directly with visitors. These events help increase brand visibility and generate new business opportunities.

d) Survey Collection
Survey collection involves gathering customer opinions, preferences, and experiences through questionnaires or digital forms. It helps businesses understand customer needs and measure satisfaction levels. The collected data supports better decision-making and service improvement. e) Consumer Feedback Programs
Consumer feedback programs collect customer suggestions, complaints, and satisfaction ratings after product usage or service experiences. Branding & Fabrication
a) Kiosks
Kiosks are compact promotional booths used in malls, exhibitions, airports, and public places to showcase products or services. They provide customers with product information, demonstrations, and promotional offers. Kiosks help increase brand visibility and customer engagement. They are an effective tool for lead generation and direct sales.
b) Exhibition Stalls
Exhibition stalls are branded display spaces set up at trade shows and exhibitions to showcase products and services. They are designed to attract visitors through creative layouts and interactive displays. Businesses use them to demonstrate products, generate leads, and network with potential clients. Exhibition stalls enhance brand visibility and business opportunities.
c) Display Units
Display units are specially designed fixtures used in retail stores to present products in an attractive and organized manner. They improve product visibility and encourage customer purchases. Display units are commonly used for new launches, promotional offers, and featured products. They help maximize retail space and enhance the shopping experience.
d) Glow Sign Boards
Glow sign boards are illuminated advertising boards installed outside shops, offices, and commercial buildings to improve brand visibility. They attract customer attention during both day and night. These signboards display the company logo, name, and promotional messages. Glow sign boards strengthen brand identity and increase store footfall.
e) Fabrication & Installation
Fabrication and installation involve designing, manufacturing, and setting up branding materials such as kiosks, display units, signboards, and exhibition stalls. The process ensures high-quality construction and professional installation according to brand standards. Proper execution improves durability, safety, and visual appeal. These services help create a strong and consistent brand presence.


Audit & Analytics
a) Retail Audits: Retail audits involve inspecting retail stores to evaluate product availability, pricing, shelf placement, and merchandising standards. They help identify gaps in execution and improve retail performance. Audit reports provide valuable market insights for brands. Regular retail audits ensure better compliance and sales growth.
b) Mystery Shopping: Mystery shopping is a research method where trained individuals visit stores as regular customers to evaluate service quality and customer experience. They assess staff behavior, product availability, and store standards. The findings help businesses improve service performance. Mystery shopping enhances customer satisfaction and operational excellence.
c) Competitor Analysis: Competitor analysis involves studying competitors' products, pricing, promotions, and marketing strategies. It helps businesses understand market trends and identify opportunities. Companies use this information to improve their competitive position. Effective competitor analysis supports better business decisions and growth.
d) Market Surveys: Market surveys collect information about customer preferences, buying behavior, and market trends through questionnaires or interviews. They help businesses understand consumer needs and demand. The collected data supports product development and marketing strategies. Market surveys improve decision-making and customer satisfaction.
e) Campaign Reporting: Campaign reporting measures the performance of marketing activities by tracking key metrics such as reach, engagement, sales, and customer response. Reports help evaluate campaign effectiveness and identify areas for improvement. They provide valuable insights for future marketing decisions. Regular reporting ensures better return on marketing investment.
f) GPS Attendance Tracking: GPS attendance tracking records the real-time location of field employees while marking their attendance. It helps verify store visits, improve workforce accountability, and monitor field operations. Businesses can track employee movement and productivity accurately. This system increases transparency and operational efficiency.
g) Live Photo Reporting: Live photo reporting involves capturing and uploading real-time images of retail activities, product displays, and promotional executions from the field. It provides visual proof of task completion and compliance. Managers can monitor field performance remotely. Live photo reporting improves transparency and execution quality.
h) ROI Measurement:  ROI (Return on Investment) measurement evaluates the financial performance of marketing campaigns by comparing the investment made with the revenue generated. It helps businesses determine the effectiveness of marketing activities. ROI analysis supports better budget allocation and strategic planning. Measuring ROI ensures maximum value from marketing investments.
